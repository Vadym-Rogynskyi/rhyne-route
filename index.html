About this planner

Rhyne Route is an independent planning tool, not affiliated with Lenoir-Rhyne University. Use it to prepare for advising, not to register.

Where the data comes from

Core (General Education) requirements: 2026–27 Undergraduate Catalog. Every major (40) and minor (55) from the same catalog's Programs of Study, with exact course codes, titles and hours (linked in the checklist). RN-completion and adult Professional Studies programs aren't included. Every start year uses this one catalog, while colleges usually hold students to the catalog of the year they enter, so a student who started earlier (or starts later) should compare their own catalog year and confirm the rule with LR's Registrar. Teams: LR Athletics.

What's estimated

The order of classes (by course number, not LR's official sequence or prerequisites), how many classes a "choose N hours" line becomes, and which concentration you follow (the plan uses the first one listed; the checklist shows them all). Program hours come from each page's stated general electives. The ✦ picks are my recommendations, not LR's.

How the plan is built

Every term stays between 12 and 18 credits. Core comes first, major classes run to your last term, minors start in year two. A class that's on both your major's list and a Core list counts once for both; confirm that with your advisor.

The Core in brief · 41 hours

FYE 100 first semester · Language & Culture · Global Challenges (lower and upper) · ENG 131 + COM 111 · HES 100 + activity · fine arts, social science, lab science, two humanities (one REL), math · LRU 410.
